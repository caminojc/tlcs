Does TNS reduce the bathroom reverb? Compare current vs TNS at 9.6k and 5k.

TNS applies a short filter along MDCT bins to push quantization noise into the loud parts of each frame and suppress it in the quiet gaps. This is how AAC/EVS/Opus avoid the "washy" transform coding sound.
